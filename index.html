About Me

I am MJ A. Aquino, a Criminology student with a dream of becoming a dedicated and courageous police officer. I chose Criminology because I want to learn more about laws, justice, and the responsibilities involved in protecting and serving the community.

My journey is guided by discipline, hard work, kindness, and perseverance. I believe that becoming a good police officer is not only about wearing a uniform, but also about having integrity, compassion, and genuine concern for others.

My Interest

A criminology student with a growing interest in crime, justice, and community safety.

This website presents my academic journey, interests, experiences, and future goals. I believe criminology is not only about studying crime—it is also about understanding people and society.

My Journey as a Criminology Student

02

My journey as a Criminology student is filled with learning, hard work, and perseverance. I chose this field because I want to understand the law, justice, and public safety, while pursuing my childhood dream of becoming a police officer.

While studying, I also work and help on the farm by taking care of plants and animals. These experiences have taught me discipline, responsibility, and patience.

My family is my greatest motivation. Despite the challenges, I will continue working hard and learning because every experience brings me closer to my dream of becoming a dedicated and courageous police officer.
